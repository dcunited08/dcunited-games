Chickens vs Pigs

🐔 pen: 0 🐷 Chickens' turn 🐷 pen: 0 🐔

Tap a piece, then tap where it should go. Jumps are required: pieces that must jump are ringed in gold. Reach the far row to become a Rooster or a Boar, who can move backwards. Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter or Space picks and drops, Esc cancels.
