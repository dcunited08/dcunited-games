How to play
  • Fill the grid so every row, column and 3x3 box has 1 to 9 once.
  • The dashed shapes are cages. The small number in a cage's corner is the total of its digits, and a cage never repeats a digit.
  • Tap a square, then tap a number. Tap the same number again to clear it.
  • Turn on Notes to jot small pencil marks. Placing a number clears it from the notes in its row, column, box and cage.
  • Red numbers break a rule. Check shows squares that differ from the answer. Hint fills in one square for you.
