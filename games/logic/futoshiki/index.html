How to play
  • Fill the grid so every row and column has each number once.
  • The signs between squares must be true. The open side of the sign faces the bigger number.
  • Tap a square, then tap a number. Tap the same number again to clear it.
  • Turn on Notes for small pencil marks. Placing a number clears it from the notes in its row and column.
  • Red means a rule is broken. Check shows squares that differ from the answer. Hint fills in one square.
